How it works

From first call to a harder target

A simple process: we listen, look at the home, recommend a plan you approve, then install it cleanly and show you how everything works.

1

Request a quote

Tell us what you'd like to protect, where you are in the GTA and which services interest you.

2

On-site assessment

We walk the property with you — doors, glass, entry points, blind spots and where cables can run.

3

Your plan

You get a written recommendation and quote. Nothing is booked until you approve it.

4

Install & walkthrough

Our installers complete the work, tidy up and show you how to use cameras, audio and alarms.

Physical first

Hardened doors and protected glass slow an intruder down — that's what buys time.

Then eyes & ears

Hardwired cameras and two-way audio let you see and speak to what's happening.

Then noise

Sirens and horns draw attention and push an intruder to leave.

A place to shelter

For households that want it, a panic or safe room gives everyone a secure place to wait.

Without Guardian Home Defence

Protected glass
