Technology & security

How Guardian Shield Films works

A clear (or privacy) polyester security layer bonds to your glass — absorbing impact energy, holding shards together, and giving you time when seconds matter.

Process

From consult to protected glass

1

Assess & measure

We review glass types, risk points, and privacy goals — then specify Clear, Mirror, or Foggy Guard for each pane.

2

Prep & apply

Technicians deep-clean the glass, apply film with professional solution and squeegee technique, then trim for crisp edges.

3

Cure & protect

The adhesive cures into a durable bond. Your windows keep their look — with shatter-resistant performance behind the scenes.

⚙
Multi-layer strength

Security-grade polyester layers distribute force across the pane instead of letting cracks run free.

🛡
Shard retention

On impact, film holds fragments together — reducing flying glass and delaying forced entry.

☀
Clarity or privacy

Choose invisible Clear Guard, daytime Mirror Guard, or soft Foggy Guard — all with the same protective core.

✓
Pro install only

Edge quality and bubble-free bonding matter for longevity — we handle application end to end.
