Service 01

Hardened & reinforced doors

Most forced entries target the door. We assess your entry points and upgrade the weak links — frame, strike, hinges and the door itself — so your front, side, back and garage-entry doors resist kicks and prying.

  • Door and frame assessment for front, side, rear and garage-entry doors
  • Frame and jamb reinforcement with heavy-duty strike plates and long screws into the framing
  • Hinge-side reinforcement and security hinge upgrades
  • Solid-core or steel security door supply and installation options
  • Lock & latch hardware upgrades installed as part of the hardening package
Get a quote

Service 02

Break-resistant glass gel protection

Glass is the easiest way in. Our break-resistant glass gel protection adds a clear protective layer that helps hold the pane together when it is struck, slowing an intruder down instead of leaving an open hole.

  • Clear finish that keeps your view and daylight
  • Helps keep glass in place on impact rather than falling out
  • Suited to patio doors, sidelights, basement and ground-floor windows
  • Can be paired with Guardian Shield Films for privacy
  • We'll recommend gel, film, or both after looking at your glass
Get a quote

Service 03

One-way security films — Guardian Shield Films

Guardian Shield Films is our window-film line. Every film adds a security layer that helps hold glass together; you choose the look — invisible, daytime mirror, or frosted.

  • Clear Guard — nearly invisible protection that keeps your view
  • One-Way Mirror Guard — daytime privacy with a reflective exterior face
  • Foggy Guard — frosted privacy for washrooms, entries and basements
  • Professional prep, application and edge trimming

Service 04

Lock & latch hardware upgrades

A strong door needs strong hardware. As part of our door-hardening work we install upgraded lock and latch hardware so the bolt, latch and strike work together with the reinforced frame.

  • Lock & latch hardware upgrades installed as part of door hardening
  • Deadbolt and latch hardware supplied and fitted to reinforced frames
  • Latch guards and reinforced strike plates
  • Smart-lock hardware options fitted during the door upgrade
  • Hardware chosen to suit your door type and how your household uses it

Service 05

Hardwired security cameras

Hardwired cameras are powered and connected by cable, so there are no batteries to recharge and the picture doesn't drop when Wi-Fi gets busy. We plan coverage for doors, driveways, yards and blind spots, then run the cable neatly.

  • Coverage plan for entries, driveways, side yards and garages
  • Wired cameras with tidy, protected cable routing
  • Local recording with remote viewing on your phone
  • Can be paired with two-way audio and sirens
  • Placement that respects neighbours' privacy
Get a quote

Service 06

Sirens & horns

Noise is one of the simplest deterrents there is. We install indoor and outdoor sirens and horns, positioned so they are heard where it matters and can be triggered as part of your security setup.

  • Indoor and outdoor siren and horn placement
  • Visible units that act as a deterrent on their own
  • Can be tied into cameras or other triggers in your setup
  • Positioned for coverage while being considerate of neighbours
Get a quote

Service 07

Two-way audio

Two-way audio lets you speak and listen at your door, driveway or yard from your phone. Greet a courier, talk to a visitor, or tell someone loitering that they're on camera.

  • Speak and listen remotely from your phone
  • Works alongside your hardwired cameras
  • Useful at front doors, side entrances and yards
  • Can be added to panic / safe rooms for communication
Get a quote

Service 08

Panic / safe room installation

A panic or safe room gives your household somewhere secure to go and wait for help. We usually start with an existing room — a closet, basement room or ensuite — and harden it.

  • Room selection and planning with you
  • Reinforced or steel door with a hardened frame
  • Interior-only locking hardware
  • Camera feed and two-way audio so you can see and communicate
  • We'll flag anything that needs permits or other trades before work starts
Get a quote
