Real protection, not just a sticker on the window.

Cameras tell you what happened. Hardening helps stop it from happening. We start with the physical weak points — the door frame, the glass beside the lock, the dark side yard — and strengthen them, then add cameras, sirens and two-way audio so you can see, hear and respond.

One team plans and installs the whole thing, so your doors, glass and devices work together instead of being pieced together from different contractors.
