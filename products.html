Guardian Shield Films · A Guardian Defence Technologies company

Security films built for real glass

Guardian Shield Films is the window-film line from Guardian Home Defence. Three options — clear protection, daytime mirror privacy, or frosted privacy — each designed to help hold glass together on impact without giving up daylight.

Clarity

Clear Guard Films

Nearly invisible multi-layer security film that preserves your view while improving shatter resistance. Ideal when you want protection without tint or a mirror finish.

  • Optical clarity for living rooms, patio doors and sidelights
  • Helps hold shards together on impact to reduce injury risk
  • Slows down smash-and-grab and break-in attempts
  • Professional install for a factory-clean edge

Privacy

One-Way Mirror Guard Films

Daytime mirroring that keeps interiors private while you enjoy the view out — plus the same security-grade layer that helps glass resist shattering.

  • Reflective exterior face when it's brighter outside than in
  • Clear outward visibility from inside
  • Security film construction for impact performance
  • Sleek modern look for street-facing windows
Ask about Mirror Guard

Frosted

Foggy Guard Films

Elegant frosted privacy that softens light and obscures views — washrooms, entries, basements — without heavy curtains, and with built-in security performance.

  • Soft diffused daylight with obscured sightlines
  • Ideal for washrooms, front-door sidelights and basement windows
  • Security layer helps keep glass intact on impact
  • Design-forward finish that looks intentional
